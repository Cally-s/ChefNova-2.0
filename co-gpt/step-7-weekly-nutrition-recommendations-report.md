# Step 7 - Weekly Nutrition Recommendations Implementation Report

## Goal

Add supportive Chef Nova recommendations to the Weekly Nutrition page using only Meal Planner data, Weekly Nutrition totals, and existing nutrition ratings.

## Files Changed

- `app.js`
- `style.css`

## Recommendation Rules

- Low vegetables: shown when the vegetable rating is `Low` and at least 5 meals are planned with nutrition data.
- Low protein: shown when the protein rating is `Low` and at least 5 meals are planned with nutrition data.
- High sugar: shown when the sugar rating is `High` and at least 5 meals are planned with nutrition data.
- Missing nutrition data: shown when `mealsWithoutNutrition >= 3` or `mealsWithoutNutrition > mealsWithNutrition`.
- Mostly empty plan: shown when fewer than 5 of 21 weekly meal slots are planned.
- No nutrition data: shown when meals are planned but none have nutrition data.
- Positive state: shown when no other recommendation is triggered.

## Priority Order

1. Mostly empty meal plan
2. No nutrition data
3. Missing nutrition data
4. High sugar
5. Low vegetables
6. Low protein
7. Positive state

Recommendations are sorted by priority and limited to 3 cards. The mostly empty plan state returns only the `Plan More Meals` recommendation.

## Meal-Plan Completion Logic

`getMealPlanCompletionStats()` counts the 21 possible meal slots across Monday through Sunday. A meal is counted as planned only when the slot contains a non-empty normalized meal entry. Servings do not increase the planned meal count.

## Missing-Data Handling

Recommendations use meals with available nutrition data only. When custom meals or meals without recipe nutrition are present, the page shows a note explaining that recommendations are based only on meals with available nutrition data.

## No-Data Handling

When meals are planned but none contain nutrition data, Chef Nova shows `Use Chef Nova Recipes` and does not show protein, vegetable, or sugar recommendations.

## Positive State

When enough meals are planned, nutrition data exists, ratings do not trigger the protein, vegetable, or sugar rules, and missing nutrition data is not high, Chef Nova shows `No Major Suggestions`.

## Action Buttons

- `Open Meal Planner` navigates to the Meal Planner.
- `Find Recipes` navigates to the Recipe Finder.
- `Find Vegetable Recipes` navigates to the Recipe Finder and searches for `vegetable`.
- `Find High-Protein Recipes` navigates to the Recipe Finder, searches for `protein`, and applies the existing `High Protein` dietary filter.
- `Find Lower-Sugar Recipes` opens the Recipe Finder without creating unsupported fake filters.

Recipe Finder controls are reset before recommendation actions apply their search/filter.

## Automatic Updates

Recommendations are regenerated by `displayWeeklyNutrition()` whenever the Weekly Nutrition page is opened or refreshed. Existing Meal Planner save, edit, delete, and serving updates already call `updateWeeklyNutritionPage()`, so recommendations update with weekly totals and ratings.

## Safe Wording

Recommendation wording is supportive and avoids diagnoses, medical claims, weight claims, and alarming language.

## Disclaimer

These recommendations are simple Chef Nova suggestions based on planned meals and available recipe data. They are not medical or professional dietary advice.

## Risks or Remaining Notes

- Weekly recommendations are estimates from recipe data and planned servings.
- Custom meals can be counted in the meal plan but cannot contribute nutrition totals until replaced with Chef Nova recipe entries.
- Lower-sugar recipe action opens Recipe Finder because there is no dedicated sugar filter in the current app.
